# Two fronts: Production and CRM

Since 2026-10-13 (the user: "uma frente de produção… e eu quero voltar com a antiga frente que a gente tinha de CRM"), the account screens have a left menu (`src/components/AppSidebar.tsx`, in `Layout.tsx`; on phones a drawer) with two sections: **Produção** (Projetos, Agentes when the local app is connected, Skills) and **CRM** (Oportunidades `/crm`, WhatsApp `/crm/whatsapp`, Clientes `/crm/clientes`, Social `/crm/social`, Instagram `/crm/instagram`, Site `/crm/site`, Prospecção `/prospeccao`, Equipe `/crm/equipe`). Inside a project the Space takes the whole screen and the menu is gone. The menu reuses the shape of the old `AppSidebar` (tag `backup/antes-space-only`), not its data: the old CRM was per workspace and the workspace layer is gone.

- **Team = the company that sells** (migration `20261013150000_crm.sql`): `crm_teams`, `crm_team_members` (roles `owner`, `admin` "Gestor", `seller` "Vendedor"), `crm_team_invites`.
