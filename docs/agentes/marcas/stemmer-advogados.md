# Stemmer Advogados model (prospect)

Scope: `src/features/stemmer/**`, `brands/stemmer-advogados/**`, `public/brands/stemmer-advogados/**` and the "Stemmer Advogados" project in the Space (created 2026-10-03).

- A **prospect, not a client**: a labor and social-security (INSS) law firm with its own house at Rua São José, 195, Bairro São José, São Leopoldo (RS), on WordPress 7.1 + Elementor 4.3 Pro (Hello theme) today, built by Xeque Lab, who probably also run its Google Ads. The firm advertises on Google, so the Home is the ad's landing page: what the firm does and where it is in the first seconds, and a short path to WhatsApp. Nothing leaves the account (no approval link, invite, WordPress connection or publish) until the user says so. - Advertising rules (Code of Ethics and Provimento 205/2021): no testimonials or concrete cases (the current site shows Trustindex reviews, one with a case result), no "especialista"/"especializado" without a proven title, no promises ("garantir seus direitos", "cobrar o que é seu", "resolve seu caso"), no self-praise ("altamente qualificados", "excelência"), no urgency hooks ("atendimento imediato", "fale agora"), no prices.
- Concept, **"o cartão-ponto"** (the time card): the firm deals with people's working time (with or without a signed card, overtime, contribution time for the INSS), so the page uses the time card's grammar. Numbered square boxes (`box()`, filled green = punched), ruled records with a 2px ink top rule, "Registro 01/02" headers and column labels (Nº · Situação · Contato). The hero states "Advocacia trabalhista e previdenciária em São Leopoldo." with the WhatsApp button, then the firm's record (Desde 1996 · Rua São José, 195 · Trabalho e INSS · Presencial e online) beside the real photo of the four lawyers in front of the white facade with STEMMER and 195. The situations are rows of two records, "No trabalho" (5) and "No INSS" (5), first-person sentences with the area below; the whole row is the WhatsApp link with the subject. No gavel, scales, statue, handshake or stock photos (the current site's stock office, construction and elderly photos do not come back).
- Fonts: Encode Sans Expanded 600 (the wide letters of STEMMER on the logo and the facade) for titles, situations, names and questions; Sora (the current site's font) for text, labels (uppercase, 0.14–0.16em), menu and buttons. Headings above 16px use Encode Sans Expanded and labels of 16px or less use Sora, so the brand pass changes nothing. The current Elementor has Google Fonts disabled (Sora is a custom font): on publishing, install Encode Sans Expanded the same way.
- Core colors: ink `#0d1114` (the logo/site black: header, situations and team bands, footer), raised `#171d21`, green `#146c43` (the single accent: buttons, punched boxes, small labels and the final call band; white on it 6.5:1), deep green `#0e5233` (button hover), light green `#86cfa6` (labels and boxes on ink), white, record paper `#f1f7f3` with rule `#c9dfd1` (records, the first-contact list, the FAQ band), body `#3a3e40` (the site's text color), muted `#5c6367`, line `#dde0e1`, on-dark `#c8cdd0` / `#949b9f`. Every color in section settings is a palette color in lowercase hex (or `rgba(255, 255, 255, a)`); WhatsApp's bright green lives only in the float's CSS.
- Bands: ink header, white hero, ink situations (records on it), white first contact, ink team, white location, record-paper FAQ, green final call (with a white form card), ink footer.
- Content 1200px inside 32/24/16px gutters; sections 120/88/64px; header 72/64px, sticky. Buttons 4px, cards 6px, photos 4px. No shadows except the mobile menu panel and the WhatsApp float.

- Motion (`src/features/stemmer/story.ts`, GSAP and ScrollTrigger from jsDelivr), one signature, "the time card is punched": the hero title, text and buttons are visible from the first paint (ad visitors, no wait for the CDN); the record (`.st-intro`) rises 14px, the photo (`.st-photo`) arrives, and the record's boxes fill green one by one (`.st-row` gets `is-punched`); on scroll, each situation row is punched as it enters; `.st-rise` blocks rise 18px once. `html.st-armed` (only on a scrolling page) empties the boxes until punched. The CSS alone is the final composition (all boxes filled); reduced motion, the Elementor editor and the Space thumbnails run nothing. Checked with `build/page.ts` + `build/cdpcheck.mjs` (Edge over CDP, desktop, phone and reduced motion): every row ends punched, nothing stays hidden, no horizontal scroll.
