# Rodas experiment (carterogunsola.com)

Scope: `src/features/rodas/**` and the "Experimento · Rodas" project in the Space (page "Home", created 2026-10-09).

- An experiment, not a client: the user asked to recreate https://carterogunsola.com/ "exatamente como está" in a new project, to see whether the Space reaches that level. The design and the motion follow the original (measured live, in Chrome and headless Edge); its name, texts, works, photos, logos and code are third-party and were not used. The content is Avence Studio's (`RO_CASES` from `AV_CASES`, `brands/avence-studio/COPY.md`): the six live cases, each with a short segment and the platform, and the site link. Never present it as the reference's site.
- Look: dark paper `#1e1e1e` (light `#d6d6d5`) with fiber, dots, a "tooth" grain that jumps 8×/s and the creases of a sheet folded in three (horizontal at 30.5% and 65%, vertical at 55.5%), all CSS on `body`, so every section thumbnail is already on paper; surface `#262626`; ink `#f7f7f7`; fills at 8%; spot `#e06a4a` only in the selection. Geist and Geist Mono (the original's font is commercial). Chrome is 40px tall with 6px corners; chips 4px with 8% fill, uppercase 12px; the reading strip is mono 10px semibold, `KEY::value`, chips hanging from the top edge.
- Theme: dark by default; the header switch sets `html[data-ro-theme=light]` and stores it in localStorage. Settings carry the dark colors; the light ones come from CSS variables (`--ro-*`), so text widgets that follow the theme carry `ro-ink`/`ro-muted`.
- Header (sticky, transparent, 96/72px): reading strip (São Leopoldo time and FPS live, `CASES::06`, `IG::@AVENCESTUDIO`), the "avence" wordmark with the square point, the nav pill (text rolls on hover, a pill runs under the link, the others dim to 40%), ⌘K search (cases and pages; Ctrl/⌘+K, the magnifier on phones) and the theme switch; on phones a drawer with the big links.
- Live presence (2026-10-09, the user asked for the arrows "como se fosse usuários"): real visitors only, never simulated. Supabase Realtime public channel `rodas:<host><path>` (no login; the app's URL and publishable key from `.env`, passed by `.space/experimento-rodas/build/live.ts` to `buildHeader({ live })`; supabase-js 2.45 from jsDelivr). Each visitor gets a random Portuguese name (feminine animal + adjective, "coruja astuta") and a pastel color for the session; the mouse is broadcast at most 10×/s as viewport fractions and drawn as an arrow with a mono 10px tag; stale arrows leave after 9 s. Presence remains active, but its `PRESENÇA::ON/OFF` chip is hidden by the user's decision on 2026-10-11; the user had already removed the `ONLINE::` chip from the strip (2026-10-09). Toasts at the bottom left ("Alguém entrou / N pessoas entraram / Alguém saiu", "N online") fold what arrives within 1.5 s. Server diffs take a few seconds; closing the tab leaves at once (`pagehide`).
- Stage (`#cases`, 100vh, pulled under the header by `.ro-header+.ro-stage`): each case is a native `.ro-case` with the cover image, the die letter and the info lines (`Nº01 / 06`, name, two chips, "Ver projeto"). The behavior script draws two linked wheels in WebGL (three.js 0.169 from jsDelivr, only the core module): covers on a wheel of radius 0.45·S on the left (S = min(height, 0.52·width), 1.3 rad per step, cards 30.3% of the width at 16:9, the neighbors turned against the wheel at 40% of their angle) and a die per case on a wheel of 0.39·S on the right, with the same arc per step and the opposite turn; dies are beveled extrusions with the letter in an iridescent chrome on the face. While moving the covers bow, split colors along the travel and get grain; neighbors dim toward the paper. Wheel (one notch = one case), drag with fling, arrows, a tap on a neighbor goes there.
- Hover on the active cover (mouse only): it bulges into a CRT screen (edges bow out, corners round) and a TV bezel appears around it (dark gap, gray body, a bright lip with a light that drifts toward the bottom right); the center text scrambles in place once.
- Click (or Enter) opens the case: the screen grows to ~60% of the width and curves with faint scanlines, the neighbors spread, the center text moves 22vw right and the dies 20% right; a band of ordered-dither "TV" dots sweeps in from the right edge and out. Esc, the wheel, a drag, the arrows or a click outside close it; "Ver projeto" still opens the site.
- Old-TV static: snow, a rolling bar and a picture slip flash on the active cover when it changes and when it opens or closes, then clear in about half a second. Phones stack: covers on top, dies at the bottom, sideways drag.
- The center text changes like a drum: letters (eyebrow and name) and whole lines (chips, button) roll up when going forward, down when going back.
- Entrance (once per session, then a short fade): paper with the wordmark at the bottom left and a 000→100 counter rising at the bottom right, following the cover textures and three.js (at least 1.5 s; a CSS failsafe hides it after 6 s), then the paper fades and the wheels spin in.
- List section ("Cases selecionados"): rows with name, a line that fills, chips that fill with ink and a link circle on hover. It is what shows without the wheels and disappears with them (`html.ro-wheels`), like the original.
- CSS alone is the final composition: case 1 in the center, its cover tilted on the left, the neighbors cut at the corners at their wheel positions, dies parked on the right, and the list. Reduced motion, the Elementor editor, pages that do not scroll (Space thumbnails) and browsers without WebGL2 run no wheels.
- Builds in `.space/experimento-rodas/build/`: `home/NN-*.ts` shipped with `ship.sh NN "label"`; `page.ts` + `cdp.mjs` (a local server for `public/` and Edge over CDP) with `roteiro*.mjs` check entrance, wheel, drag, touch, theme and search.
- Page "Rodas" in the Avence Studio project (2026-10-09, the user: "Traga esse teste… exatamente como está aqui", "Quero manter o design que temos aqui"): the same three sections, with the Rodas look, not the Avence one. The Avence brand is on in that project and would change 152 settings (Geist to Inter Tight, the Rodas colors to the Avence palette), so each section carries `data.pinned` with every setting the brand would change (the Space's "manual tweak over the brand"); with the pins the brand pipeline returns the sections unchanged. `.space/experimento-rodas/build/levar-avence.ts` (run with `.space/avence-studio/build/run.mjs`) computes the pins, checks the result and fills the target files; `page-avence.ts` + `cdp.mjs` (`RO_PAGE`) + `estilos.mjs` compare the computed font, color and background of every element with the Rodas page (0 differences, desktop and phone). A section rebuilt for that page needs its pins recomputed before the push, or the brand rewrites the new settings. The experiment project stays as it is.
- Not done: the original's other pages (About, Lab, Writing, Studio, Contact) and the "what's new" bell. `cdp-presenca.mjs` checks presence with two Edge instances (arrow, count, join and leave toasts); `roteiro-abrir.mjs` checks hover, open, close and static.
