# Fonseca & Lorenço model (prospect)

Scope: `src/features/fonsecalorenco/**`, `brands/fonseca-lorenco/**`, `public/brands/fonseca-lorenco/**` and the "Fonseca & Lorenço" project in the Space (created 2026-10-03).

- A prospect, not a client yet: a law firm in the Centro of São Leopoldo (RS), Rua Bento Gonçalves, 1487, focused on labor law, plus social security (INSS), civil and advisory work for companies. The Home in the Space is a sales piece for a redesign of their WordPress + Elementor site (WordPress 5.8, WPBakery, Revolution Slider and a 2016 accounting theme). Nothing is approved by the firm.
- Sources of truth: `brands/fonseca-lorenco/DESIGN.md` and `brands/fonseca-lorenco/COPY.md` (every fact with its URL, interpretations and open items kept apart, plus the OAB advertising review). Contact data lives in `FL_CONTACT` (`src/features/fonsecalorenco/tokens.ts`). Never invent names, OAB numbers, dates, results or reviews: the site names no lawyer, the founding year is open (the site says experience since 1990, the seal says "Desde 1992") and the official WhatsApp is open (the current hero button points to (51) 9805-0413, everything else to (51) 98168-3468).
- OAB rules (Código de Ética arts. 39–47, Provimento 205/2021): informative and sober copy; no promised results, superlatives, comparisons, prices, free consultations, testimonials or concrete cases; "especialista" only with a proven title; the society name and its registration (OAB/RS 4689, from the firm's 2019 digital card) stay on the page; accounting never appears as a service next to law. The Google rating (4.2, 64 reviews) is a discreet line in "Onde fica" and an open item.
- Concept, the highlighter ("marca-texto"): labor law explained in the words of the person who works. Warm paper, graphite text, and the seal's gradient (`#E5F064` → `#F2DD53` → `#FFCA6A`) behind one or two words per title (`mark()` in `elementor.ts`, class `fl-mark`). Practice areas are first-person situations ("Fui demitido e quero conferir a rescisão"), not a list of legal branches. No gavels, scales, extra columns, handshakes or stock photos; the temple belongs to the real logo only.
- Fonts: Archivo 800 for titles (tight, −0.02em), Public Sans for text, labels, menu and buttons (buttons in sentence case, 700). Both are on Google Fonts. The DESIGN.md has no label role.
- Core colors: graphite `#282828` (the seal), footer `#1E1E1E`, card on graphite `#323230`, amber `#FFB404` (hover `#FFC53A`) for buttons with graphite text (8.3:1), dark amber `#8A5A00` for small amber text on paper, paper `#F7F5EF`, white cards, line `#E6E1D6`, body `#4A4944`, muted `#6B6962`, on dark `#C9C7C0` / `#A8A69F`. One yellow family: the gradient marks, the amber is clicked. Amber is never small text on paper. WhatsApp green only on the floating button. The old theme gold `#C09431` does not come back.
- Bands: paper hero with a faint ruled-paper texture, white situations, graphite "Como funciona" (like the firm's Instagram arts), paper timeline, white location with the native `google_maps`, paper FAQ (nested accordion), a final band filled with the seal gradient (graphite text and buttons), graphite footer. On graphite the marked words become a full-height block with graphite text (`selector.fl-on-dark .fl-mark`).
- Content 1160px inside 32/24/16px gutters; sections 112/88/64px. Buttons 4px, cards, map and photo 8px. No shadows except the mobile menu and the WhatsApp float.
- Logo: the real stacked logo (seal + white name) in the footer; in the header the seal (44px, 38px on mobile) beside the name in native text. Files: `logo/fonseca-lorenco-logo-branco.png` (original, white name), `logo/fonseca-lorenco-logo-preto.png` (our derivative with a graphite name, pending the official file) and `logo/fonseca-lorenco-selo.png`. The file name states the logo's ink (`branco`, `preto`): the Space brand reads "escuro"/"claro" in a file name as the logo's color, which swapped the footer logo for the dark-ink one when the files were named "fundo-escuro"/"fundo-claro". Logo images keep an explicit width.
- Photos: only the facade (480×360, from the current site's "Escritório" page). Rights and newer team and office photos are open items; without them, use typographic composition, never stock.
- Templates are native Elementor trees built with `src/features/fonsecalorenco/elementor.ts`; the Home's section builds are in `.space/fonseca-lorenco/build/home/`. The only HTML widget is the behavior-only motion widget, the first child of the hero.
- Motion (`src/features/fonsecalorenco/story.ts`, GSAP and ScrollTrigger from jsDelivr): one signature, the highlighter passing. Hero items rise 14px, then the gradient sweeps behind "palavras simples"; each section title's mark sweeps once as it enters (on graphite the word appears with it); `.fl-rise` blocks rise 16px once; the timeline track fills with the gradient while scrolling and lights each date (`--fl-fill`, `fl-armed`, `is-lit`). The CSS alone is the final composition (full marks, full track, everything visible). Reduced motion, the Elementor editor and the Space thumbnails run nothing. No Elementor entrance animations anywhere.
- The brand is saved and on in the Space; applying it to the Home changes 0 settings (checked with `.space/fonseca-lorenco/build/brandcheck.mjs` on 2026-10-03). Keep timings and words like "aparece"/"sobe" out of a DESIGN.md heading about motion (the reader turns them into motion rules), and diff `applyBrand` against every page before editing the front matter.
