# Evermind experiment (BYQ evermind-hero-2)

Scope: `src/features/evermind/**`, `public/brands/evermind/**` and the "Experimento · Evermind Hero" project in the Space (created 2026-10-03).

- An experiment, not a client: the user asked to craft the BYQ section "Animated Hero with Scrolling Gallery" (`https://app.byq.supply/sections/evermind-hero-2`, Evermind™ template by BYQ® Studio, Webflow) exactly as it is, effects and refinement included. The design, photos and template copy belong to BYQ: never publish them as a real site.
- The design is fixed and measured from the source (CSS, IX2 interactions and the Figma SVGs): paper `#F4F3EA`, ink `#1A1A17`, lime `#B6E400`, pill `#E6E8DD`; Merriweather 400 (h1 60/64 −2.5px) and 42dot Sans 500 (both on Google Fonts); content 680px; tiles 568/672 × 480 with 8px corners; glass widgets with 16px corners, 48% paper, inset hairlines and backdrop blur. Text widths match the original to the tenth of a pixel; keep them that way.
- Content is a parameter (`src/features/evermind/content.ts`): `EVERMIND_CONTENT` is the template copy (page "Evermind original", the reference); `SUPERELEMENTS_CONTENT` is the Superelements copy from `brands/superelements/COPY.md` (page Home). When the user said "aplique a marca da superelements" they meant the content only: "o site poderia ficar igual" (2026-10-03). Do not restyle the design to another brand unless asked; the Superelements brand is saved in the project but off.
- No invented facts in the Superelements content: the widgets show the product (the agent, the real publish steps, a publish in progress), never client results, testimonials or metrics.
- The second button keeps a real person photo, as in the original (the user found the page thumbnail wrong, 2026-10-03), so it invites a conversation: "Fale com a gente" → `/contato`.
- Story (9:16, 60 fps, no text): `.space/experimento-evermind-hero/build/story.mjs`, the site in a screen on the site's warm gray `#E6E8DD` with its dot grid. Never a pure black background (the user asked).
- Motion (`src/features/evermind/motion.ts`, a behavior-only HTML widget, first child of the section, no library): entrance blur 12px → 0 with opacity at 0.1/0.4/0.6 s, 0.5 s each; the marquee moves its own width every 20 s like the original but loops seamlessly (the original jumped back); photos are 120% of the frame and move −15% → 0% across the viewport with IX2's 50% per-frame smoothing. Button hovers are CSS only (background layer scales to 95%, label rolls 1.5em with a `text-shadow` copy). Reduced motion, the Elementor editor and the Space thumbnails run nothing.
- The glass widgets are native containers (the source had text-as-paths SVGs). Only the gauge arc is an image (PNG); the "84%" of the source uses old-style figures that Merriweather on Google Fonts does not have, so it renders with lining figures.
