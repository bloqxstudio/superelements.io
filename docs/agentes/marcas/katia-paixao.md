It **advertises on Google**: Google Ads tag `AW-435350764` and Tag Manager `GTM-NTJW5RMB` on the home, plus ad landing pages (`/previdenciario-geral/`, `/previdenciario-geral-2/`, `/trabalhista-geral/`, `/trabalhista-gestante-demitida/`, `/planprev/`) credited to "Pixel Agência de Marketing Digital". The Home in the Space sells the redesign of the home only; nothing leaves the account (no approval link, invite, WordPress connection or publish) until the user says so. Publishing must keep both tags and the WhatsApp click conversion (every button stays on `wa.me/5551991209364`). Sources of truth: `brands/katia-paixao/COPY.md` (every fact with its URL, the OAB review, the ads findings and the open items) and `brands/katia-paixao/DESIGN.md`.
- Public facts only. The site writes "Kátia", the logo, Google and Instagram write "Katia": the page uses "Kátia Paixão Advocacia" until the office decides. CNPJ and legal name are not public; the old "Paixão & Goulart Advocacia" logo (OAB/RS 8.530, 2023) is not used. "Especialista", "15 anos de experiência", the team ("nossa equipe"), the hours and the official e-mail are open items; never invent them. Contacts sit in `KP_CONTACT` (`src/features/katiapaixao/tokens.ts`); the Google rating (4.8) is the user's reading, shown only as a discreet line in "Onde fica".
- Advertising rules (Code of Ethics and Provimento 205/2021): informative and discreet; no promised results or speed ("garanta", "recupere o que é seu", "resultados", "imediatamente", "sem compromisso"), no testimonials (the current site's Google review widget does not come back), no prices, cases or comparisons. Write "atua em"; "especialista" only after the office confirms the title. Kátia's own sentence ("Sei que estou cuidando de vidas, de histórias, de sonhos.") is her statement, not a testimonial, and appears in the lawyer section.
- Concept, **"a barra do K|P"**: the logo is K and P split by a thin vertical bar, and her studio photos are half wine, half cream with her on the seam. The hero is that monogram: a wine field with what the office does and where, a sand field, and her portrait on the seam; the wine field (`.kp-field`) and a champagne pinstripe (`.kp-seam`) live inside the portrait frame (`.kp-frame`), positioned at the photo's own wine/cream split (53.9% of its width, `KP_PHOTOS.seam.split`), so the page's seam continues the photo's. The practice areas are **phases of life** (from the site's "Proteja seus direitos em qualquer fase da vida"): I Enquanto você trabalha, II Quando a saúde pede uma pausa, III Perto da aposentadoria, IV Quando a família muda; the phase sits left of a 1px wine bar and its first-person situations sit right, each opening WhatsApp with the subject. "Longe do Brasil" (Brazilians abroad, 100% online) is its own wine-deep band. No gavel, scales, statue, columns, handshake or stock photos (the current site's stock photos and flags do not come back).
- Built for ad visitors: what and where in the first screen, WhatsApp in the header (icon button on phones), the four ad subjects as chips under the hero title ("O INSS negou meu benefício", "Planejar a aposentadoria", "Fui demitida grávida", "Divórcio ou inventário"), every situation with its own WhatsApp subject (`kpWhatsApp(subject)`: "Olá, Dra. Kátia! Vim pelo site e gostaria de conversar sobre …"), and the floating WhatsApp button.
- Fonts: Italiana 400 (thin high-contrast display, like the K|P) for titles, phases, names and her sentence, never below 28px; Manrope for text, situations (600, always in `text-editor` widgets so the brand pass keeps Manrope), labels (uppercase, 0.16em, with the bar in front), menu, buttons and the FAQ questions (styled by CSS, because a custom accordion title typography would become Italiana under the brand). Italiana's figures are old-style: numbers (address, phone, dates) go in Manrope; phase numerals are Roman.
- Core colors: wine `#7e1a26` (hero field, buttons on light, small labels on light, 9:1 on linen), wine deep `#5c1420` (footer, abroad band, wine button hover), champagne `#ddc68d` only on wine (bars, labels, the hero and final buttons with wine-deep text, 7.9:1; hover `#e8d6a8`), linen `#f6f1ea` (page), sand `#e3dad6` (the photos' cream: hero right field, lawyer and location bands, the documents card), ink `#2e2022` titles, body `#4a3a3c`, muted `#6b5a5b`, line `#ddd2c8`, on wine `#f6f1ea` / `#dcc0bf`. Every color in section settings is a palette color in lowercase hex; the transparent value is `rgba(246, 241, 234, 0)` (the palette has no white); WhatsApp green lives only in the float's CSS.
- Content 1200px inside 32/24/16px gutters; sections 120/88/64px; header 72/64px, sticky. Buttons 3px (the brand's button radius), photos 2px, cards and the map 4px. No shadows except the mobile menu panel and the WhatsApp float; no textures.
- Logo files (recolored from the public files' alpha, smoothed): `logo/katia-paixao-logo.png` / `-claro.png` (horizontal signature, 1096 × 174, footer 300/260px) and `logo/katia-paixao-monograma.png` / `-claro.png` (K|P, 640 × 483, header 54/44px, final call 76px), always with explicit width; the name next to the header monogram is native text. The vector logo is an open item.
- Photos: `fotos/katia-retrato-barra.webp` (hand at the chin, on the wine/cream split, hero) and `fotos/katia-retrato-sorriso.webp` (smiling, arms crossed, lawyer section), 466 × 582 crops of the site's 1920 × 700 banners (the site's CDN serves 1600px), so never wider than about 440px. - Templates are native Elementor trees built with `src/features/katiapaixao/elementor.ts`; the Home's section builds are in `.space/katia-paixao-advocacia/build/home/` (shipped with `build/ship.sh`). Checked by code and with a CDP harness (`build/harness.ts`, `build/cdpmotion.mjs`): every hidden item ends visible and the field's clip-path clears.
