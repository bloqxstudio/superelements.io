# Planta & Obra model (prospect, Estúdio Norte design system)

Scope: `brands/planta-e-obra/**` and the "Planta & Obra" project in the Space (page "Luiza 343", created 2026-10-13; builds in `.space/planta-obra/build/`).

- A **prospect, not a client**: construtora e incorporadora at Rua São Joaquim, 611, Centro, São Leopoldo (RS), on WordPress + Elementor 3.20 Pro today (plantaeobraadm.com.br). Nothing leaves the account (publish, approval link, invite, WordPress, message) until the user says so. The old "Home" in the project (red, Barlow Condensed) was the first sales piece; "Home (site atual)" is the faithful import.
- **Design system: the Estúdio Norte** (https://estudionorte.framer.website/), chosen by the user on 2026-10-13 ("Esse é um ótimo ds para usarmos como base… footer e navbar e páginas e componentes… Quero usar esse design system"). Source of truth: `brands/planta-e-obra/DESIGN.md`. Ink `#0C0C0B`, raised `#151513`, paper `#E9E6DF`, muted `#9C978D` (`#5E5A53` on paper), hairlines paper at 16%/34%, the logo red `#73160D` only in small marks. One family, Google Sans Flex: big titles 500 at −0.06em, list titles 700, text 400, 12px labels uppercase at 0.18em, paper buttons with ink 700 text and 2px corners. Content 1824px inside 48/32/18px gutters; sections 160/112/80px. Elementor 3.20 does not list Google Sans Flex, so the header's behavior widget also loads it with a `<link>`.
- Logo (`logo()` in `build/po.ts`, the site logo cropped from LOGO-P_O.png): in the header, the paper-toned version with no plate (the user tried the colored one on a paper plate and asked for the paper one back, 2026-10-13: "tava melhor"); in the footer, the colored one (red and gray) on a small paper plate. The files are named `po-marca-papel.png` / `po-marca-cor.png` on purpose (a file name with "logo" is swapped by the brand pass).
- Page "Luiza 343" (the case): real texts, renders, plans and construction photos of https://plantaeobraadm.com.br/luiza/ (photos from their WordPress in the sizes it already generates, mapped by name in `IMG()`; the plans are local WebP inlined by the push. WebP copies in `public/` were tried and broke outside the local server, because the published app did not have the files). Order: Cabeçalho (sticky, transparent over the hero, ink bar after it), Abertura (façade photo, the lede, and the giant "LUIZA" filled + "343" outlined, whole and centered: the user's request; the outline is a 3px stroke painted under an ink fill, `paint-order: stroke fill`, because the variable font shows its internal overlaps with a plain stroke), Apresentação (the site's paragraph in big type, Luiza signature, four facts), Capítulos (four full-screen sticky covers that stack; supporting labels at the top and the name and description centered, the user's request: at the bottom they were hard to read while scrolling), Diferenciais (Norte numbered list), Galeria (12 renders in a horizontal track pinned on desktop), Plantas (paper band), Andamento da obra (87%, nine stage bars, 14 photos from Maio/23 to Maio/26 that wipe from bottom to top with the scroll, pinned on desktop), Contato (the site's form fields), Rodapé (the brand name outlined and centered), WhatsApp flutuante (green circle fixed bottom-right on the whole page, the user's request).
- Motion (`build/motion.ts`, GSAP 3.13 + ScrollTrigger + Lenis from jsDelivr, one behavior-only widget, first child of the header): hero intro (photo settles from 1.12, letters rise one by one), photo parallax, words of the statement light up with the scroll, covers shrink to 92% and darken as the next one covers them, rows draw their rule while each title and then its text light up word by word (the user asked for the statement's reveal on the differentials, to show less at once), gallery and construction use a tall section with a CSS-sticky stage instead of the ScrollTrigger pin (the pin jolted on release, 2026-10-13), each section asks for its photos two screens ahead (the gallery's off-screen slides too) and each photo fades in once decoded, bars fill and numbers count once, `.po-rise` blocks rise 28px once. CSS alone is the final composition; reduced motion, the Elementor editor and non-scrolling pages (Space thumbnails) run nothing.
- Brand in the Space: the Norte DESIGN.md is saved but **off** (2026-10-13), because turning it on would restyle the old Home; applying it changes 0 settings in the 10 Luiza sections (`build/brandcheck.ts`). The old Home got the old brand's few tweaks written into its own settings, so it looks the same with the brand off. Turning the Norte brand on is the user's call, together with redoing the Home in the new system.
- Open items: rights to the renders and photos (theirs, on their site), the office phone (the site footer says (51) 3559-2412, Google and the WhatsApp link say 3592-2412; the page uses 3592-2412), the form's destination e-mail, the date of the 87% figure (the page shows it next to the Maio/26 photo), and a vector logo.
