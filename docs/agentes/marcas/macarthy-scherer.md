# Macarthy Scherer model (prospect)

Scope: `src/features/macarthyscherer/**`, `brands/macarthy-scherer/**`, `public/brands/macarthy-scherer/**` and the "Macarthy Scherer" project in the Space (created 2026-10-03).

- A real law firm in São Leopoldo (RS), **not a client yet**: the Home in the Space is a sales piece for the redesign (same WordPress + Elementor they already run). Two partners: Gláucia Macarthy (family and succession, OAB/RS 82.685) and Marcelo Scherer (criminal, OAB/RS 96.494); Rua Bento Gonçalves, 673, sala 701, Centro. Sources of truth: `brands/macarthy-scherer/COPY.md` (every fact with its URL, the OAB advertising review and the open items) and `brands/macarthy-scherer/DESIGN.md`. Never invent numbers, years, awards, names, hours, prices or credentials.
- OAB advertising rules (Code of Ethics and Provimento 205/2021) apply to every text: informative and discreet; no promised results, superlatives, comparisons, prices, "free consultation" hooks, testimonials or concrete cases. "Especialista" only with the exact formal title (Gláucia's is in Direito Processual Civil, not family law): elsewhere write "atua em". Each partner always appears with the OAB number. The Google rating (4.9, 120 reviews) is a discreet seal pending the firm's confirmation.
- Concept, "a conversa reservada": calm, light and personal. The firm's name is the two partners, so both appear in the hero with area and OAB; areas are written as life situations, each column with its partner and that area's WhatsApp; the first contact is explained in three steps with professional secrecy stated plainly. No legal clichés (scales, statue, gavel, columns, handshake, stock photos) and no navy-and-gold gradients.
- Fonts: Newsreader 400 for titles, names, questions and numerals (one italic emphasis word at most); Instrument Sans for text, labels (uppercase, 0.18em), menu and buttons. Headings above 16px use Newsreader and labels of 16px or less use Instrument Sans, so the brand pass changes nothing.
- Core colors: ink `#0a243b` (logo ink: titles, primary button, first-contact band, footer), logo blue `#074468` (final call band, button hover), bronze `#bb8553` (the current site's accent: hairlines, numerals, small marks; text only on ink, never on the logo blue), bronze ink `#8a5a2c` for small labels on light, paper `#f6f4f0`, white, mist `#e8eef2` (FAQ band), body `#3e4a56`, muted `#5e6a75`, line `#dce1e5`, on-dark `#c3d0db`. Every color in section settings is a palette color in lowercase hex (or `rgba(255, 255, 255, a)`); WhatsApp green lives only in the float's CSS.
- Content 1200px inside 32/24/16px gutters; sections 120/88/64px. Pill buttons (999px, the brand's button radius), cards and the map 16px, portraits 12px. Cards are white with a `#dce1e5` hairline (white at 14% on ink). No shadows except the mobile menu panel and the WhatsApp float.
- The logo's thin tapered line ("o traço", between MACARTHY SCHERER and ADVOGADOS) is the only ornament: eyebrow hairlines, the bronze line under the two partners in the hero, the vertical thread of the first-contact steps and the wide line in the final call.
- Logo: vector SVGs from the current site, `logo/macarthy-scherer.svg` (blue, on light; header 190/150px) and `macarthy-scherer-branco.svg` (white, on dark; footer 220/190px), always with explicit width. The "SM" favicon and the "Scherer Macarthy" files are not used.
- Photos: only the two partner portraits from the current site (Gláucia's is 255px, so never show it wider than about 260px); usage rights and new photos are open items. The old photo of Marcelo in front of an award backdrop is not used.
- Templates are native Elementor trees built with `src/features/macarthyscherer/elementor.ts`. The mobile menu and the FAQ are nested accordions, the map is `google_maps`; the only HTML widget is the behavior-only story script, first child of the hero.
- Motion (`src/features/macarthyscherer/story.ts`, GSAP and ScrollTrigger from jsDelivr): hero items rise 14px and fade in, then the bronze line draws from the center under the two partners and "Advogados" fades in; the first-contact thread draws with the scroll (scrub); `.ms-rise` blocks rise 18px once. The CSS alone is the final composition; reduced motion, the Elementor editor and the Space thumbnails run nothing. Containers keep `transition-property` without transform, and no Elementor entrance animation goes on anything GSAP moves.
- Brand in the Space is saved and on: applying it changes 0 settings in the 10 Home sections. The DESIGN.md front matter holds only colors, two font families, the button radius, the logo and the two portraits; keep timings and words like radius, shadow or hover out of its body, and diff `applyBrand` (`.space/macarthy-scherer/build/brandcheck.ts`) before editing it.
