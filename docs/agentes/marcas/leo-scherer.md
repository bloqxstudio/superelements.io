# Leo Scherer model

Scope: `/leoscherer-style-guide`, `/leoscherer-elementor-preview`, `/leoscherer-redesign`, `/leoscherer-redesign-proposta`, `src/features/leoscherer/**`, `src/features/space/leoschererTemplate.ts`, `src/pages/LeoSchererRedesign*`, `public/leoscherer/**`, `docs/site-imports/leoscherer/**`, `brands/leo-scherer/**`, `public/brands/leo-scherer/**` and the "Leo Scherer" project in the Space.

New version in the Space (2026-10-02, "Home" page; "Site atual (importado)" is the faithful reference): same brand and logo, organized as an Apple-standard editorial showcase. Sources of truth: `brands/leo-scherer/DESIGN.md` and `brands/leo-scherer/COPY.md` (site and Instagram facts, open items). Builders: `src/features/leoscherer/redesign.ts` and `story.ts`; section build files in `.space/leo-scherer/build/home/`.

- Contact and purchase go to Instagram @leooscherer, as on the site (no WhatsApp on record). "Simule" and "Inscreva-se" use the site's Elementor popup links (ids 2432, 9064, 3993); they only work published on the LS WordPress.
- Helvetica only; content 1200px inside 32/24/16px gutters; sections 120/88/64px; pill buttons; cards 20–24px. Red `#FF0000` only for the dot before "Simule" and small labels on dark. One light band: "Em destaque" (white, product photos multiplied on `#F5F5F7` frames).
- Backgrounds (the user asked for dots and soft lights, Apple elegance): each section has a native `.ls-bg` layer with a masked 24px dot grid and two or three heavily blurred lights. Keep the lights soft: the user found the blue too strong (2026-10-02). Never name a container class `ls-light` (it is the light).
- Hero (the user asked for a phone animation): centered copy over the iPhone 18 Pro pair; the phone rises tilted in 3D and settles, a glint masked by the phone's own image sweeps it, and on desktop the hero pins briefly while the copy leaves and the phone grows to the center. Official Apple images with black backgrounds are converted to transparent PNGs; never use `mix-blend-mode` on a stage GSAP moves (it showed a border under the pin).
- Hover only on the featured products (the user's decision): the image itself zooms inside its frame and "Ver produto" appears; the frame never turns white (scaling the wrapper isolates the multiply). No hover effects on the other cards.
- "Novos e seminovos" cards put the phone beside the copy (on top on mobile), never below it (the user disliked it).
- Images load as WebP (2026-10-03, 8.9 MB → 0.9 MB): `lsImg()` swaps `.png`/`.jpg` for the `.webp` that `.space/leo-scherer/build/webp.cjs` writes next to each original, at twice its displayed width. A new image needs that script and a push before the published app (hostingersite) serves it; until then it shows broken there. The script also writes each WebP's size to `src/features/leoscherer/imageSizes.ts`, and `image()` reserves that ratio (`aspect-ratio:auto W/H`), so the page never grows while photos arrive and ScrollTrigger measures the right positions.
- Loading (the user found it dry and broken, 2026-10-03): `LS_LOAD_SCRIPT` (`story.ts`), a behavior-only widget that is the header's first child, keeps each photo hidden until it is fully loaded and decoded, then fades it in (opacity only, 0.8 s; GSAP zoom and drop-shadow filters stay). A failed photo never shows the broken icon. Opening texts (`.ls-intro`, delays `ls-i1`…`ls-i5`) rise with CSS from the first paint, without waiting for the CDN; the hero phone waits for its photo and GSAP (2.6 s at most, then it just fades in). The desktop pin waits for those entrances to end, because wrapping the hero restarts CSS animations (`ls-done` marks finished ones). What is already on screen when ScrollTrigger arms is never hidden again. Light pages set `html{background-color:#fff}`: a classic inline script waits for the external stylesheets, and the browser paints the black canvas meanwhile.
- Hero glint, Apple style (the user saw it "loading broken", 2026-10-03): the phone rises first with the wine light turning on behind it (0 → .55, the scroll's starting value), and the glint sweeps once only after the phone has settled (1.15 s). `.ls-glint` is a container, so it needs `transition:none`: Elementor's background transition made the band sweep the empty mask before the phone arrived (a white ghost of the phone). `.ls-device` has the photo's width (653 px, max 100%), so the stage never collapses while the photo loads (on mobile the next section used to jump 500 px).
- "Experiência LS" shows the client's latest Instagram reel (Box Launch Edition 2026) in a 9:16 phone frame, by the user's request: a 15 s silent highlight loop plays by itself like a GIF, "Toque para ativar o som" restarts the full video from the beginning with sound, and a small "Com som / Sem som" pill toggles mute. No native video controls. From page load the same player floats as a card in the bottom-right corner (200 px, 118 px on mobile) with a clear white × ; inside the section it returns to the frame. The × stops the video and hides the card for the rest of the visit (sessionStorage). Transitions are slow and smooth (the user found them too fast): the video flies between the frame and the corner (FLIP, 750 ms, ease-out), the first appearance rises and fades in (800 ms) and the × lowers and fades it out (450 ms); with reduced motion it switches directly. Keep GSAP rise and any transform off the frame's ancestors: a transform breaks the fixed card. Behavior lives in a behavior-only HTML widget in that section; without JS the sound button opens the reel on Instagram.
- Purchase simulator (2026-10-03, the user's request): four screens (product, down payment with trade-in, card, installments) with the site's real rates from `simulate.php` (`src/features/leoscherer/simulator.ts`, `COPY.md` › Simulador de parcelas: total = value ÷ (1 − rate), up to 6x interest-free, 18x). It opens in a side panel from the right ("na lateral", the user asked) from any `#simulador` link (`LS_LINKS.simulator`), and comes with the product of the screen already selected: the closest `.ls-sim-ctx` (hero: `ls-sim-ctx-name`, `-img`) or the product page's `.ls-sim-ctx-page` (`-name`, `-spec`, `-price`, `-tag`, `-img`); a product without a price (iPhone 18 Pro) asks for the value. The section "Simulador (abre na lateral)" (`build/simulador/lateral.ts`) sits before the footer of Home, Categoria and Produto; without the script, in the Elementor editor and in the canvas thumbnails it stays in the page. The "Simulador" page (`/simulador`, `s02-simulador.ts`) is the standalone version. Content is native (cards, the form widget for values, 18 installment tiles); the behavior-only HTML widget is the section's last child. Inside the fixed panel keep `flex-wrap:nowrap` on the containers: Elementor wraps them on mobile and the parts went side by side.
- Simulator panel, the user's follow-ups (2026-10-03): compact and organized (480px, a one-line header with a round ×, steps without numbers, products as a list with price and condition on the right, Continuar across the footer) and no red anywhere in the simulator (it didn't match the page). On the last screen the summary card (installment, total and every line: product, value, down payment, financed value, trade-in, card) stays pinned under the header while the installments scroll, and the close button is green in the footer: "Fechar em 12x no WhatsApp" (#25D366 with dark text), a `wa.me` link whose message carries the summary and follows the chosen installment. The LS WhatsApp number is still missing (`LS_WHATSAPP`, `COPY.md`); never invent it. Sticky parts of the panel need `position:sticky!important`: BASE_CSS sets `position:relative` on the root's direct children.
- Footer ends with the price disclaimer: no large logo. A 3D LS mark (layered, diagonal, blue-tinted steel) was tried on 2026-10-03 and the user had it removed; the faint LS mark before it is gone too.
- More buttons (the user asked, 2026-10-03): cards and tiles use small outline pill buttons with an arrow (`cta()` in `redesign.ts`), not text links. On the Watch photo tile the button gets a dark translucent backing.
- Inner pages as examples (2026-10-03): "Categoria · iPhones novos" (dark hero with breadcrumb, lineup and category pills, white product grid) and "Produto · MacBook Neo", which is light by the user's request (white and `#F5F5F7` bands, black and outline buttons; only the header and footer stay black). Product cards everywhere use `productCard()` + `PRODUCT_CARD_CSS`, with the featured-products hover. Products, prices and colors are static text from the site; in WordPress they become WooCommerce templates.
- Instagram photos are 360×640 post covers: keep them small (mosaic, service cards, JBL tile) until the client sends originals and confirms use.
- "Vitrine em curva" (2026-10-05, from three reference images the user sent; on "Página 6", build `.space/leo-scherer/build/home/p3-vitrine.ts`): white band, `#F5F5F7` product cards (3:4, 24px, photos multiplied) on a concave 3D arc: the center card faces front, the side ones turn 14° inward and sit back 19% of the card width, and x compensates the perspective so the gaps stay even. Name, condition and spec at the top, price at the bottom left, a thin "+" (product page; LS has no cart) at the bottom right. Real products and R$ prices from COPY.md instead of the reference's USD. The behavior-only script (first child) loops the cards, drifts slowly (slower under the mouse), drags with inertia, gets a gentle push from the page scroll, and the arrows, arrow keys and Tab focus bring a card to the center. Without the script (and in the Elementor editor) it is a flat snap strip; reduced motion and non-scrolling pages (Space thumbnails) show the arc still. Helvetica, not the reference's serif (LS brand). Checked with `build/vitrine/page.ts` + `cdp.mjs`.
- Vitrine card opening (2026-10-05, the user: "um motion abrindo o card em forma expandida"; I chose the card itself over a separate modal): a click or Enter on a card brings it to the center of the arc (440 ms), then a copy of the card inside the native `.ls-cv-modal` shell grows from the card's rect (clip-path) to a 1080×700 panel (full screen with the photo on top on phones) over a dark blurred veil. Photo, name, spec and price slide to their new places (FLIP), the "+" turns 45° into the "×" in the corner, and the facts, buttons and price note rise after (700 ms; closing 540 ms by ×, Esc or the veil, focus back on the card). The facts (warranty, trade-in from iPhone 7, 18x, same-day delivery) are site text by condition; buttons "Comprar pelo Instagram", "Simule as parcelas" (the page's simulator with this product via `.ls-sim-ctx`; hidden when the page has no `#simulador`) and "Ver detalhes". They live natively in each card as `.ls-cv-more`, hidden in the arc. Reduced motion opens and closes without the transition. Checked with `build/vitrine/cdp-modal.mjs` and `cdp-modal-lento.mjs` (animations at 0.1×): focus trap, scroll lock, side card, veil. The frame loop reads `performance.now()`, not the rAF stamp.
- The brand is saved in the Space but off: applying it recolors the light band and labels (157 changes on 2026-10-02).

- Source: https://leoscherer.com.br/ (WordPress + WooCommerce + Elementor). The public sitemap contains 2,094 product URLs and nine product categories, represented by home, category and product template families.
- Fonts measured from the source: Helvetica for display/editorial text, Source Sans Pro for Storefront body copy and Inter only in the agency credit. Helvetica has no transported font file; use `Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif` and document the destination fallback.
- Core colors: canvas `#000000`, raised `#101010`, hero navy `#151C25`, paper `#FFFFFF`, theme body `#6D6D6D`, soft `#B6B6B6`, WooCommerce purple `#7F54B3`, action blue `#6EC1E4`, signal red `#FF0000`.
- Desktop content is approximately 1120px with 20px gutters; tablet changes at 1024px and mobile at 767px. Mobile gutters are 16px.
- Hero type is 52/52px weight 600 on desktop; Watch and story headings are 40/40px; the immersive statement is 75/75px. Body follows the source's 16/25.888px Source Sans Pro rhythm.
- The language is a black editorial stage: isolated product renders, long negative-space bands, one white AirPods band and sparse blue/red accents. Do not turn it into a generic rounded ecommerce grid, bright SaaS page or glass interface.
- Gradients are source-derived radials only: `#151C25` to `#010101` in hero/history and `#0B1B33` to `#00040A`/black in financing. Radii and shadows are rare.
- Assets are local under `/leoscherer/assets`; do not hotlink uploads or copy analytics, pixels, WooCommerce sessions, cart, checkout, payment or account scripts.
- Templates are native Elementor trees. Search, gallery, video and newsletter use supported widgets; the newsletter has no destination action until configured in the target WordPress.
- Hover never carries essential content. Controls scale to 0.96 on press; motion is guarded by `prefers-reduced-motion`.
