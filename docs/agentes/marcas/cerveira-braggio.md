# Cerveira Braggio model (prospect)

Scope: `src/features/cerveirabraggio/**`, `brands/cerveira-braggio/**`, `public/brands/cerveira-braggio/**` and the "Cerveira Braggio" project in the Space (created 2026-10-03).

- A **prospect, not a client**: the real-estate law office of Andreza Cerveira Braggio (OAB/RS 129.181) at Rua São Caetano, 410, Centro, São Leopoldo (RS), on WordPress 6.8 + Elementor 4.1 (Hello theme) today (braggio-lawyer.com, a one-page ad landing). The Home in the Space sells the redesign; nothing leaves the account (no approval link, invite, WordPress connection or publish) until the user says so. Sources of truth: `brands/cerveira-braggio/COPY.md` (every fact with its URL, the OAB review of the current site and the open items) and `brands/cerveira-braggio/DESIGN.md`.

- Advertising rules (Code of Ethics and Provimento 205/2021): informative and discreet; no promised results or speed ("evite prejuízos", "resolução ágil", "atendimento imediato"), no self-praise ("altamente capacitados", "vasta experiência"), no prices, testimonials, cases or comparisons. Write "pós-graduada em Direito Imobiliário"; "especialista" only after the office confirms the title. Buttons speak of "a advogada", never "um advogado".
- Concept, **"a planta"** (the floor plan): real-estate law drawn as a technical drawing. White paper, warm black ink, a faint graph-paper grid and the logo's gold on dimension lines. The hero is a drawing sheet ("Folha 01") with Andreza's cut-out portrait over the grid and a dimension line (`cota()`) with her name; the practice areas are six rooms of a floor plan (ink walls drawn by the grid gaps, a door opening and swing arc per room), each a first-person situation with the area underneath and a WhatsApp link with the subject; the lawyer's facts sit in a title block ("o carimbo", "Folha 02"); the first contact is a chain of dimension lines; the final call is a last sheet with crop marks. No gavel, scales, statue, handshake or stock photos (the current site's stock buildings do not come back); the column lives only inside the real monogram.
- Fonts: Marcellus 400 (the logo's Roman capitals) for titles, situations, questions and the name; IBM Plex Sans for text, annotations (uppercase, 0.14–0.16em, tabular figures), menu and buttons. Headings above 16px use Marcellus and labels of 16px or less use Plex, so the brand pass changes nothing.
- Core colors: ink `#1d1b18` (titles, primary button, plan walls, first-contact band, footer), raised ink `#2a2723`, gold `#cfa354` (dimension lines, annotation squares, gold button with ink text 7.4:1; text only on ink), light gold `#ddb866` (gold button hover), gold ink `#7a5c1e` for small labels and the hero accent on light, paper white, stone `#f0efeb` (situations, FAQ, the hero sheet), body `#45423d`, muted `#6b675f`, line `#dad7d0`, on-dark `#d3cec4` / `#a9a397`. Every color in section settings is a palette color in lowercase hex (or `rgba(255, 255, 255, a)`); WhatsApp green lives only in the float's CSS.
- Content 1200px inside 32/24/16px gutters; sections 120/88/64px; header 76/64px, sticky. Everything square (buttons, cards, photos, map: radius 0, the brand's button radius). Plan walls 2px inside and 4px outside; hairlines 1px. No shadows except the mobile menu panel and the WhatsApp float.
- Logo: only the gold monogram `logo/cerveira-braggio-monograma-dourado.png` (transparent, works on white and ink), always with explicit width (40/34px header, 30px title block, 46px footer, 64px final call), with the name as native Marcellus text beside it. `logo/cerveira-braggio-logo-dourado.png` (full logo) is kept but unused.
- Photos: `fotos/andreza-retrato.webp` (cut-out, hero) and `fotos/andreza-escritorio.webp` (at the desk, lawyer section, cropped 4:5 on desktop and 4:3 below 1025px), both from the current site; usage rights are an open item. No captions.
- Templates are native Elementor trees built with `src/features/cerveirabraggio/elementor.ts`; the Home's section builds are in `.space/cerveira-braggio/build/home/` (shipped with `build/ship.sh`). The mobile menu and the FAQ are nested accordions, the map is `google_maps`; the only HTML widget is the behavior-only story script, first child of the hero.
- Motion (`src/features/cerveirabraggio/story.ts`, GSAP and ScrollTrigger from jsDelivr), one signature, "the plan draws itself": hero items rise 14px; the sheet frame traces clockwise (`.cb-frame-*`), the grid wipes in from the left (`.cb-sheet-grid`, clip-path), the portrait rises 24px and the name's dimension line grows from the center (`.cb-hero-cota`); on scroll the floor plan wipes in once from the left and the rooms fade in (`.cb-plan`, `.cb-room-in`); `.cb-rise` blocks rise 18px once. The CSS alone is the final composition; reduced motion, the Elementor editor and the Space thumbnails run nothing; whatever GSAP moves has `transition:none` (BASE_CSS) and no Elementor entrance animation. Checked by code and with a CDP harness (`build/harness.ts`, `build/cdpshot.mjs`): every hidden item ends visible and the plan's clip-path clears.
- Brand in the Space is saved and on: applying it changes 0 settings in the 10 Home sections (2026-10-03, `.space/cerveira-braggio/build/brandcheck.ts`). The DESIGN.md front matter holds only colors, two font families, the 0px button radius, the monogram and the two photos; keep timings, radius, shadow and hover words and imagery headings out of its body, and diff `applyBrand` before editing it.
