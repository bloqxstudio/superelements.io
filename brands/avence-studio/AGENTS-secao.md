### Avence Studio model (our studio)

Scope: `src/features/avence/**`, `brands/avence-studio/**`, `public/brands/avence-studio/**` and the "Avence Studio" project in the Space (pages "Home" and "Identidade", created 2026-10-03, rebuilt 2026-10-04).

- **Our own studio, not a client**: Avence Studio, São Leopoldo (RS), Instagram @avencestudio. It sells the service (a new site delivered ready, charged once, and an optional monthly plan). The Home is the studio's new site (avencestudio.com is a plain HTML page today; it moves to WordPress) and, since 2026-10-04, **a studio portfolio**: the cases are the center, the positioning is a studio focused on speed (the user's words), and the craft must show in every detail. **Superelements never appears on it** (the user keeps the two brands apart). Sources of truth: `brands/avence-studio/DESIGN.md` (with the identity decision) and `brands/avence-studio/COPY.md` (facts, the cases with their URLs, the excluded ones, open items).
- **Cases** (`AV_CASES` in `src/features/avence/cases.ts`): six live sites. **ProcessBase** comes first (the user's request, 2026-10-04: processbase.com.br, WordPress + Elementor, our own build); then the five from the current site's carousel that are live: Vizor Películas (Astro), Flávia Neto, Zena Viagens, Contplan, PrexParts (WordPress + Elementor). Infinity Day and Tankker CRM are out (not live at the addresses found). The hero gallery shows all six (`track([0…5])`); the Identidade page's case post stays on Vizor. Each case says only what the site shows: name, segment, city when the site says it, what the page has, platform. No numbers, results or testimonials in our text. Full-page captures by `.space/avence-studio/build/capture.mjs` (Edge over CDP, scrolls first so lazy images and Elementor entrances load, hides cookie banners), converted with ffmpeg to WebP in `public/brands/avence-studio/cases/`: `-capa` 960×600, `-pagina` 1200 wide (up to 4800px of page), `-celular` 520 wide; every image reserves its ratio with `aspect-ratio`. A site with a scroll story (ProcessBase) is captured with `AV_BLOCK=gsap` (the CSS final composition), `AV_CSS` to close the gaps a sticky stack leaves in a still photo, and `AV_SWAP` for images broken on the live site. Client authorization for each case is an open item. The São Leopoldo law offices are not clients and never appear.
- **Identity, evolved on 2026-10-04** (the user: "Podemos evoluir a identidade", "use uma fonte serifada para os destaques"; earlier symbol attempts were rejected, and so was an "a" whose bowl is the blue square: "podemos manter como estava antes o logo"): the logo is the word **"avence"** in Instrument Serif with a **blue square point** (where the two old arrows met) and small "STUDIO" in Inter Tight caps; the symbol is the "a" with the point. Files (outlines, no embedded font) in `public/brands/avence-studio/logo/`, generated by `.space/avence-studio/build/gen-identidade.cjs`; `avence-marca-anterior.png` is the old logo for the before/after. File names for alternative lockups must not contain "logo" (the Space brand swaps such images): the stacked ones are `avence-studio-empilhada-*`. Header and footer write the signature as native text (`.av-wm`, `av-pt`, `av-studio`).
- **Type**: **Inter Tight** everywhere (titles 500, tight −0.03 to −0.045em; text 400; labels 500 and buttons 600 in tracked caps). **Instrument Serif italic** only on highlights (`<em class="av-em">`, one word per title, blue; light blue on ink), the signature and the case counter (`av-serif`). The serif is never in an Elementor font setting (the brand pass would swap it): it lives in CSS and is loaded by a `<link>` in the hero's behavior widget (and in the first section of "Identidade").
- **Colors**: paper `#f3f1ec`, white, stone `#e6e2d9`, ink `#111111` (the old site's), raised ink `#1b1b1b`, point blue `#2b3cf0` (buttons with white 7:1, highlights, small labels on paper, the "Recebeu" band), deep blue `#1f2cc4`, light blue `#8f9bff` (on ink), mist `#e4e7fd`, body `#3a3833`, muted `#66625a`, line `#dcd8cf`, on-dark `#d8d5cd` / `#9d998f`, dark hairline white at 14%. No lime, no mono font, no dot grid, no app screens (all Superelements). Every color in section settings is a palette color in lowercase hex (or `rgba(255, 255, 255, a)`); WhatsApp green only in the float's CSS; each case's glow color lives in the cases section CSS.
- **Grain and halftone** (the user: "um noise na página toda… como se tivesse viva"; the first halftone was found too strong, "precisa ser muito mais suave"): the behavior script adds a fixed full-page grain layer (`.av-grain`, SVG feTurbulence, 6.5%, ten-step jitter per second, static with reduced motion; never in the editor or on non-scrolling pages). Halftone textures (`public/brands/avence-studio/texturas/`, fine 9px grid at 45°, from `.space/avence-studio/build/gen-halftone.cjs`) are native container backgrounds (`halftone()` in `elementor.ts`, class `.av-ht`), only in a few corners (hero, the blue band, contact; the cases section has none since 2026-10-04: its dark background stays plain) at 10–24% with gradient masks. No crop marks around the gallery any more (the user: "não precisa das <>").
- **Glass menu**: at the top the header is the paper band; after 24px of scroll (`html.av-scrolled`, set by the script even with reduced motion) it detaches into a rounded glass bar (20px radius, 18px on phones; side padding 36px before the signature and 14px after the CTA, 24/10px on phones, item spacing unchanged (the user asked for side padding, not between items); concentric 10px CTA corners; paper at 62%, `backdrop-filter: blur(22px) saturate(1.7)`, inner white hairline, layered soft shadow, spring easing `cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1)`); the bar is 1000px wide at most. Solid paper where backdrop-filter is unsupported. The header keeps a fixed flow height (73px, 65px at ≤1024px, `overflow: visible`) and the bar grows over the page: when the sticky header grew from 73 to 110px on scroll, everything below moved 37px after ScrollTrigger had measured, and the cases pin released 37px off.

- **Motion** (`src/features/avence/story.ts`, GSAP and ScrollTrigger from jsDelivr, one behavior-only HTML widget, first child of the hero, which also loads the serif): the cases in motion. **Entrance** (redone 2026-10-04 by the better-ui brief: easier to see, ease-out only, no bounce, interruptible), once per session (sessionStorage `av-intro`), only with the page at the top and no hash, about 2s: a paper overlay built at first paint (no wait for the CDN; shapes from `src/features/avence/mark.ts`, generated by `gen-identidade.cjs`, including the header geometry) (1) reveals the whole logo "avence STUDIO" with a bottom-up mask (`clip-path` inset 100%→0 and 16px rise, 0.6s, CSS keyframes); (2) the blue point arrives last (scale .25→1, opacity 0→1, blur 4px→0, 0.35s from 0.5s); (3) from 0.85s the logo flies and shrinks into the header as a shared element (0.9s `power3.out`; `expo.out` covered most of the path in 0.1s and read as a jump), landing on the real header wordmark measured by its baseline (a Range on the first letter and the bottom of the inline-block `.av-pt`), then cross-fades with it while the paper fades; (4) from 1.1s the hero parts enter 80ms apart (label, title, text, buttons: 20px rise, opacity, blur 4px→0, 0.5s); (5) from 1.35s the visible gallery tiles rise 32px left to right (stagger capped at 0.3s in total), and at 1.7s the gallery starts moving from zero speed. A tap, key or wheel speeds the timeline 4×. A 3.2s failsafe releases everything. The CSS keyframe fill must be cleared (`animation: none`) before GSAP takes over an element. Hero: the headline is visible from the first paint; two rows of bare screens (three identical groups each) run in opposite directions at 56px/s (40 on phones), starting from zero speed after the entrance, JS-driven on the GSAP ticker, draggable with mouse or finger (pointer events, `touch-action: pan-y`, inertia, a drag never becomes a click), slowing to about a fifth under the mouse; a soft blue light follows the mouse; the hero halftone moves in parallax. Cases on desktop (≥1025px): the section pins (`pinSpacing: true`) and each case fills the screen; per case, scrolling first moves the site's page down inside the browser frame, then the case recedes (scale .96, 30% opacity, no blur) and the next one enters with a slight 3D turn, the phone rising and the text arriving; counter, bar and index follow (timeline `onUpdate`). The last case holds still for a short beat (0.4 of a step) and the pin releases exactly where it stood: the pinned section is `min-height: 100vh` (not a fixed height), and the section itself, not only its children, drops Elementor's `transform .4s` transition (`selector,selector .e-con{transition-property:background,border,box-shadow}` in `BASE_CSS`), otherwise ScrollTrigger's release transform animated and the section slid over the next one. Below 1025px: no pin, the track is a native scroll-snap strip (the next case peeks; "Deslize para o lado"), and the pages autoplay inside the frames. Phone screens always autoplay while visible and pause under the mouse. `.av-rise` blocks rise 18px once. CSS alone is the final composition (static gallery, horizontal strip, top of each site); reduced motion, the Elementor editor and non-scrolling pages (Space thumbnails) run nothing; whatever GSAP moves has `transition:none`; no Elementor entrance animations. Checked with `build/page.ts` + `build/cdpintro.mjs` (entrance frames, drag, glass menu; desktop and phone), `build/cdpcases-sem-entrada.mjs` / `cdpmob.mjs` / `cdpexit.mjs` (pin release step by step: section top 0 at the end, gap 0 to the next section, at 1280×720, 1440×900 and 1920×1080) (Edge over CDP, desktop and phone: pin length, track steps, inner page scroll, counter, nothing hidden at the end, no horizontal scroll) and `space video` (frames extracted with ffmpeg, frame by frame at 30fps).
