## First-slice positioning

### Main idea

Marketing that remains inside the company: direction, people, process, technology and governance.

### Working definition of marketing construtivo

“Marketing construtivo” is currently a proposed articulation, not a definition found verbatim on the source site. Working definition:

> Construir, dentro da empresa, as pessoas, os processos, a tecnologia e a rotina de gestão necessários para que o marketing deixe de depender continuamente de terceiros.

Henrique must approve the term and definition before publication.

### Tone

- Direct, precise and practical.
- Short sentences and meaningful contrasts.
- Founder voice may be warmer and more personal, but should stay grounded in execution.
- Prefer “capacity, structure and autonomy” over blanket attacks on agencies.

## Offer facts from the public site

- Six months of implementation.
- Three subsequent months of follow-up.
- Three phases: diagnosis and direction, construction, consolidation.
- Deliverables include organization design, role profiles, recruitment support, onboarding, task/agenda/documentation/AI systems, management rituals and result monitoring.
- Explicit fit: companies that sell through Inside Sales.
- Application-led commercial flow; public pricing is absent.
